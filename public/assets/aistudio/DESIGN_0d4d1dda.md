## Brand & Style

This design system targets high-speed local commerce and on-demand delivery, bridging three interdependent personas: customers craving speed and visual appetite appeal, merchants requiring ruthless order dispatch clarity, and couriers operating under dynamic, one-handed mobile constraints.

The design philosophy combines **Modern Functionalism** with **Warm Tactility**:
- **Appetite and Proximity:** Anchored by an energetic, deep warm citrus orange (`#FF6500`), balanced by rich organic cream (`#FFFDFB`) in light mode and deep espresso-tinted charcoal (`#121110`) in dark mode, avoiding clinical coolness in favor of local warmth.
- **Content & Hero Photography:** Food imagery and merchant branding drive hierarchy. Interface elements act as supportive framing through soft rounded cards and pill shapes rather than intrusive boundaries.
- **Rule of Single Focus:** Exactly one primary warm orange CTA per screen state to eliminate cognitive hesitation during high-intent checkouts and instantaneous courier assignment decisions.
- **Tone & Voice:** Direct, welcoming, transparent, and low-friction. Microcopy emphasizes delivery precision via transparent interval estimates rather than speculative single-minute arrivals.

## Colors

The color system operates on dual parallel architectures (Light and Dark) engineered to preserve contrast, legibility, and warmth in variable outdoor lighting conditions.

### Brand & Interactive Hierarchy
- **Primary (`#FF6500` / `#FF7600`):** Reserved strictly for key transactional triggers (Add to Cart, Confirm Order, Accept Dispatch), active bottom navigation indicators, and active map routes.
- **Interactive States:** Shifts to `--brand-hover` on pointer focus and `--brand-pressed` during active touch feedback.
- **Brand Soft Surfaces:** Light mode utilizes `--brand-soft` (`#FFF1E8`) and dark mode utilizes `--brand-dark-soft` (`#3A2112`) for badge fills, selected tags, and counter chips without visually competing with primary buttons.

### Background & Surface Stacking
- **Light Mode:** Foundation canvas is `--bg-light` (`#FFFDFB`), elevating to pristine `--surface-light` (`#FFFFFF`) for cards and floating sheets, with `--surface-alt-light` (`#F6F0EA`) reserved for input fields and unfocused filter buttons.
- **Dark Mode:** Deep warm charcoal `--bg-dark` (`#121110`) elevates to `--surface-dark` (`#191817`) and `--surface-soft-dark` (`#211E1B`). Harsh pitch black (`#000000`) and cold slate grays are avoided to keep the orange accent grounded and tactile.

### Status & Feedback Semantics
Semantic statuses remain strictly insulated from brand orange:
- **Success (`#22A35A` / `#2BBE6C`):** Merchant open indicators, order delivered states, and verified ratings.
- **Warning (`#D97706` / `#F59E0B`):** Preparation bottlenecks, peak surge alerts, and expiring courier offer timers.
- **Error (`#D92D20` / `#F04438`):** Out-of-stock items, invalid payment states, and critical cancellation actions.
- **Info (`#3B82F6` / `#60A5FA`):** Order notes, courier instructions, and legal metadata.

## Typography

The typographic expression is powered by **Plus Jakarta Sans**, providing high geometric clarity, open apertures, and friendly geometric terminals that balance transactional rigor with consumer approachability.

### Typographic Hierarchy & Scanning Rhythm
- **Headlines & Titles:** Set with heavy weights (`700` and `800`) using tight negative letter spacing (`-0.015em` to `-0.02em`) to guarantee quick recognition on smaller mobile displays.
- **Body Text:** Standardized to max 2–3 lines in lists and cards (`body-md` and `body-sm`) with a strict line-clamp rule to preserve rhythm during vertical feeds.
- **Numeric & Pricing Emphasis:** Price figures, ETA readouts, and countdown metrics utilize `title-sm` or `headline-md` weights to ensure instant visual capture.
- **Brand Text Discipline:** Extended paragraphs and multi-sentence descriptions must never be rendered in primary brand orange; orange text is reserved exclusively for single-line badges, active links, and tab selections.

## Layout & Spacing

Layout and spatial rhythm are rooted in a strict **8px base unit system**, guaranteeing consistent vertical alignment and natural touch targets.

### Screen Partitioning & Spatial Ratios
- **Mobile Foundation:** Designed primarily for a `390px` mobile viewport, adapting fluidly down to `360px` and up to `430px` with fixed `margin-mobile` of `16px` (`1rem`).
- **Touch-First Target Principle:** Any interactive element (button, icon action, stepper increment, chip, bottom navigation destination) maintains a minimum bounding box of **44px × 44px**.
- **Live Tracking Viewport Distribution:**
  - Dynamic Map Canvas: Consumes **60% to 70%** of the active mobile viewport height.
  - Interactive Tracking Bottom Sheet: Anchored to the lower **30% to 40%**, elevating on user drag for expanded milestones.
- **Desktop/Tablet Scaling:** On viewports exceeding `768px`, the layout shifts to a centered shell with a max-width of `1280px`. Cards reflow into a dynamic 2-to-4 column grid, preventing horizontal image stretching.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual depth is achieved through intentional tonal container shifts paired with warm, short ambient drop shadows. Heavy glassmorphism and aggressive multi-stop blurs are banned to keep performance high and content readable.

### Layering Hierarchy
1. **Level 0 (Canvas):** `--bg-light` (`#FFFDFB`) / `--bg-dark` (`#121110`). The infinite scrolling canvas.
2. **Level 1 (Default Containers & Feed Cards):** `--surface-light` (`#FFFFFF`) or `--surface-dark` (`#191817`). Supported in light mode by an ultra-soft drop shadow (`0 2px 8px -2px rgba(17, 17, 17, 0.06)`) combined with a hairline border (`#E9E2DC`). In dark mode, drop shadows are omitted entirely; contrast is achieved strictly via the border (`#3A342F`) and surface step.
3. **Level 2 (Floating Action Banners, Bottom Sheets, Search Bars):** Elevated floating elements receive a distinct ambient projection (`0 8px 24px -4px rgba(17, 17, 17, 0.12)`) in light mode, signaling temporary z-index superiority over the feed.
4. **Level 3 (Modal Overlays & System Dialogs):** Scrim backing utilizing warm black tint (`rgba(18, 17, 16, 0.6)`) with subtle `4px` backdrop blur strictly limited to isolating background distraction.

## Shapes

The interface embraces a rounded, modern aesthetic:

- **Cards & Primary Modules:** Uniform `16px` border-radius (`rounded-lg`) applied to all product cards, merchant highlights, order summary panels, and courier dispatch blocks.
- **Inputs & Search Components:** Set to `12px` or `14px` border-radius, creating subtle differentiation from outer cards while remaining comfortable for thumb interaction.
- **Pill Primitives (`999px`):** Applied to category selection chips, status badges, counter buttons, and compact filter toggles, maximizing horizontal scan efficiency.
- **Bottom Sheets:** Top-left and top-right radii set to `24px`, creating an organic physical sheet metaphor when pulled upward from the bottom nav boundary.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary CTA:** Full-bleed or fixed button with `--brand` fill (`#FF6500` / `#FF7600`), pure white text, bold label (`label-lg`), height `48px` (minimum `44px`), and `12px` or `999px` corner radius. Shows active scale feedback (`scale(0.98)`) and shifts to `--brand-pressed` when tapped.
- **Secondary CTA:** Surface fill `--surface-alt` (`#F6F0EA` / `#28231F`) with primary text color (`#111111` / `#FAF8F5`) and subtle border.
- **Destructive CTA:** Solid or tinted background using `--error` with distinct high-contrast white text.

### Chips & Filter Controls
- **Category Chips:** Pill-shaped (`rounded-full`), `36px` to `40px` height. Inactive state: `--surface` background with hairline border and `--text-secondary`. Active state: `--brand-soft` background with `--brand` text and a `1.5px` border in `--brand`.
- **Sponsored Badges:** Subdued rectangular badge with `4px` radius, subtle background, and uppercase `caption` text reading "PUBLICIDAD" in `--text-secondary`.

### Cards
- **Merchant Card:** `16px` border-radius, clean photographic banner (16:9 ratio) topped with a floating rating pill (`★ 4.8` in `--success` or black with yellow accent) and delivery time badge (`20-30 min`). Body area contains merchant title, category tags, delivery fee, and a subtle favorite heart toggle.
- **Product Card:** Compact horizontal layout for lists (image `80×80px` with `12px` radius) or vertical grid card with hero image. Includes product title, 2-line truncated description, price in bold, and a quick `+` button in a `36px` circular or rounded container.
- **Courier Offer Card:** Priority card featuring prominent pickup/dropoff points, transparent estimated earnings, distance in kilometers, and an active `60s` visual countdown progress bar using `--warning` or `--brand`.

### Form Fields & Inputs
- **Search Bar:** Pill or `14px` radius container in `--surface` or `--surface-alt`, featuring an inline search icon, placeholder text in `--text-secondary`, and an optional right-aligned filter button.
- **Form Controls:** Checkboxes and radio buttons enforce minimum `20×20px` visual boxes with `44×44px` invisible touch target pads, filling with `--brand` when active.

### Lists & Steppers
- **Order Tracking Stepper:** Dual-leg vertical or horizontal progress stepper (Leg 1: Store confirmation & prep; Leg 2: Courier dispatch to door). Completed segments use `--brand`, active segments pulse, and upcoming segments stay in `--border`.
- **Cart & Quantity Controls:** Stepper pill containing minus (`-`), numeric count, and plus (`+`) buttons, styled in `--surface-alt` with bold text.

### Navigation Elements
- **Bottom Navigation Bar:** Fixed at the base of the viewport (`56px` to `64px` height + safe area inset), white or dark charcoal surface with a thin top border. Exactly 5 destinations:
  1. *Inicio* (Storefront home)
  2. *Explorar* (Category search & filters)
  3. *Pedidos* (Active order tracking & history)
  4. *Ofertas* (Promotions & discount feed)
  5. *Perfil* (Account, address book, wallet, and mode switch)
- **Top App Bar:** Clean header providing a real-time delivery location pill with dropdown chevron, merchant/app wordmark, and notification icon bell with unread badge dot.
